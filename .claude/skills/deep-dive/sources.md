# Deep-Dive Source Catalog

Curated upstream sources that lead the sell-side/consensus. Many are paywalled —
use free posts, press releases, podcast show-notes, X/Twitter threads, conference
talks, and search snippets; note when blocked. **Always include layer H (primary)
and layer I (skeptic).**

> Note: SemiAnalysis and Fabricated Knowledge **merged** (2026) — now one team.

## A · Semiconductors / AI infrastructure / hardware
- **SemiAnalysis** — semianalysis.com / newsletter.semianalysis.com (Dylan Patel). Gold standard: AI datacenter, compute economics, supply chain, foundry, packaging. Paywalled but high-signal free posts.
- **Fabricated Knowledge** — fabricatedknowledge.com (Doug O'Laughlin). Semis financial + tech; cycle analysis.
- **SemiWiki** — semiwiki.com. EDA/IP/foundry industry, design-side, lots of free articles + forum.
- **More Than Moore** — morethanmoore.substack.com (Ian Cutress, ex-AnandTech). CPU/architecture/packaging detail.
- **Asianometry** — asianometry.com + YouTube. Deep semi history & tech explainers (lithography, materials).
- **Irrational Analysis** — irrationalanalysis.substack.com. Semis, candid.
- **Mule's Musings / Tech Fund** — mule.substack.com. Semis/AI hardware.
- **TechInsights** — techinsights.com. Teardowns, process-node analysis (paid).
- **Mercury Research** — x86/GPU/ARM unit & revenue share (cited widely; press snippets).
- **Dell'Oro Group** — delloro.com. Networking/datacenter switching/optics market share.

- ⚠️ **FinancialContent / "finterra" syndicated deep-dives** — markets.financialcontent.com. Structured, information-dense company deep-dives (surfaced strongly for both LITE & ARM), but AI-syndicated — use as a fast scaffold, **verify every specific claim** against primary/specialist sources. *(found via LITE/ARM)*

## H · PRIMARY sources (always pull — earliest layer)
- **Earnings transcripts** — FMP `earningsTranscript`; **Motley Fool** (fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts, free full text); also Quartr, earningscall.biz. Management frames the thesis first. *(Fool found via ARM)*
- **SEC filings** — FMP `secFilings` + SEC EDGAR. 10-K business + risk factors; 8-K material events; S-1 for IPOs.
- **Investor days / conference decks** — company IR pages.
- **Hyperscaler capex** — FMP financials of MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN/META/ORCL (demand signal for infra names).
- **Industry conferences** — Hot Chips, ISSCC, OFC (optical), GTC (NVIDIA), CES, JPM Healthcare (biotech), Analyst Days.

## I · Skeptic / short-seller (MANDATORY — feeds the bear slot)
- **The Bear Cave** — thebearcave.substack.com (Edwin Dorsey). Short/skeptic research + research-tool roundups.
- Activist/short shops: Muddy Waters, Kerrisdale, Spruce Point, Grizzly, Hunterbrook (hntrbrk.com).
- **Seeking Alpha** bear articles (FMP news surfaces these); short-interest data via FMP.
- Always run an explicit "bear case / what breaks this" search before finalizing.
- ⭐ **For any IPO < ~12 months old: the S-1/F-1 "Risk Factors" section is the single richest bear source** (customer concentration, lock-up, related-party/CFIUS, going-concern). SEC EDGAR by CIK. *(found via CBRS — Cerebras: G42/MBZUAI concentration + CFIUS were all in the S-1)*
